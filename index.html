Criterion 01 / Body of work

Responsibility

Technical authority for privacy-aware signal infrastructure supporting Meta's global advertising and AI systems.

Platform-wide standardsPrivacy-aware AI infrastructure
S

SituationEvolving privacy regulation and platform policies changed how advertising systems could use first- and third-party data. Meta needed to preserve value for businesses while ensuring global privacy and policy compliance.

T

TaskRepresent the First-Party Data organisation and define a scalable, privacy-compliant strategy for how signals were produced, governed, and consumed.

A

ActionArchitected high-throughput distributed processing, separated identity-bearing source data from model-consumable aggregates, and established standards for signal definitions, validation, freshness, monitoring, alerting, and ownership.

R

ResultThe architecture and standards are used across the first-party signals platform, supporting risk detection, AI-safety modelling, and advertising-intelligence workloads while creating a consistent, governed approach to signal development and integration.

Criterion 03 / Additional criterion

Invention & innovation

A new point-in-time recovery architecture for Microsoft Teams messaging data at enterprise scale.

$1.5M annual savings25% of global traffic supported
S

SituationMicrosoft Teams needed a reliable recovery mechanism for business-critical messaging data. Repeated full-copy backups duplicated substantial data and created an inefficient recovery model at scale.

T

TaskDesign a scalable capability that could restore messaging data to a selected point in time while reducing reliance on repeated full-copy backups.

A

ActionCombined incremental data reconstruction with distributed task orchestration, independently tracked and retriable work, partition-aware workflows, and production observability using C#, Cosmos DB, and Microsoft's distributed task infrastructure.

R

ResultPoint-in-Time Restore introduced a new recovery path, strengthened resilience, improved recovery efficiency, and reduced annual backup-storage costs by approximately $1.5 million.
